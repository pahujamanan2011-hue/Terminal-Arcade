4. Local Storage Notice

Terminal Arcade stores high scores locally in your browser using the Web Storage API (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

The following keys are stored in your browser's local storage:

  • terminalarcade_nightcircuit_hi — Night Circuit high score
  • terminalarcade_startrail_hi — StarTrail high score
  • sw_hi — Slappy World high score
  • sw_dist — Slappy World best distance
  • sl_hi — Signal Lost high score
  • vs_hi — Void Serpent high score
  • bf_hi — BlockFall high score

This data is stored entirely on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data or local storage for this domain. It is never transmitted anywhere.
