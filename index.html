> HOW TO PLAY [click to expand]
RETRO BALL W / S or Arrow keys to move paddle. First to 5 wins. D = change difficulty.
VOID SERPENT WASD or Arrows to steer snake. Eat apples. Collect power-ups. Walls wrap.
BLOCKFALL A/D = move, W = rotate, S = soft drop, SPACE = instant drop. Clear lines to score.
NIGHT CIRCUIT A/D or Arrows to steer. Avoid traffic. Collect NOS & Turbo power-ups. Coins = +20.
STARTRAIL SPACE / W / ↑ to jump (double jump available). Collect fragments. Avoid obstacles.
SLAPPY WORLD SPACE or tap to flap. Fly through gaps. Collect crystals and energy cells.
SIGNAL LOST Click the correct answer button before the timer runs out. 3 mistakes = game over.
ALL GAMES P = pause  |  F = fullscreen  |  ESC = back to menu  |  ← BACK button = exit game
